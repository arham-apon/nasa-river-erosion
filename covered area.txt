Our pipeline covers the middle reach of the Jamuna, from around the Jamuna (Bangabandhu) Bridge in the south up to Gaibandha in the north.

**Bounding box (`AOI_BBOX` in `config.py`)**
- Latitude: 24.40° N to 25.30° N, about 100 km north to south
- Longitude: 89.45° E to 89.95° E, about 50 km west to east

**Districts and upazilas along this stretch**
- **West bank:**
  - Sirajganj (Sirajganj Sadar, Kazipur)
  - Bogura (Dhunat, Sariakandi)
  - Gaibandha (Saghata, Fulchhari)
- **East bank:**
  - Tangail (Bhuapur)
  - Jamalpur (Sarishabari, Madarganj, Islampur, Dewanganj)

**Not covered**
- **North of the box:** the upper Jamuna in Kurigram (Chilmari, Roumari, Rowmari). Several of CEGIS's 2026 predicted hotspots are there.
- **South of the box:** the lower Jamuna below the bridge, toward Aricha and the Padma confluence.

**To include Kurigram,** extend the northern edge:
```python
AOI_BBOX = [89.45, 24.40, 89.95, 25.60]
```
Then re-export all years with the new box. The grids must match across years, so you can't mix old and new exports.

The paper itself studied the Jamuna more broadly. Our box is a smaller section chosen to keep processing fast for the hackathon.
